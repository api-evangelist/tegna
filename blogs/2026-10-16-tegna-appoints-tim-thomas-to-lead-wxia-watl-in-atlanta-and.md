Thomas moves from president and general manager of TEGNA’s Tampa and Jacksonville stations McLean, Va. — TEGNA Inc. (NASDAQ: NXST) today announced that it has appointed Tim Thomas to lead WXIA, the company’s NBC affiliate in Atlanta, its sister station, WATL, and WMAZ, the CBS affiliate in Macon.
